Settings

Forecast mode — per year Each year builds its Sales Forecast independently. Pick a year, then choose how that year is forecast — e.g. leave 2026 on Original but run Seasonality with a growth % on 2027.
Shared model settings (all years) These tune how the Seasonality & Target models behave for every year that uses them. Past weeks are never changed; seasonal peaks are calibrated from real sales history and last year's peak‑season stock‑outs are corrected.
Warehouse capacities
Landed cost estimate Assumptions used to estimate each product's future landed cost (shown on the plan next to its Landed Cost). Estimate = FOB + sea freight per item → convert to £ → add duty → add inland haulage per item. Freight/haulage per item = the rate below ÷ how many units fit in a full container (container CBM ÷ the product's CBM).
Weeks Cover colour bands Weeks Cover estimates how many more weeks current stock will last (past weeks use actual sales, future weeks use the forecast and include incoming arrivals). Each value range below is shaded in the plan's Weeks Cover row — aim for a green "sweet spot" around your target, with too‑low (stock‑out risk) and too‑high (overstock) flagged.
The rebuy algorithm proposes additional orders to keep each Live product in stock, consolidated into ~container loads and timed by supplier lead time (China ~11 wks, non‑China ~3). On a forecast year it can place orders arriving in the first weeks (placed in the prior year's tail). These settings tune the cover it targets and how containers are grouped.
Weeks cover by season Override the cover target per product season. Raising e.g. Summer builds more buffer as summer demand ramps, helping capture sharp in‑season spikes (blank = use the default above).
One product per container For suppliers whose MOQ is a full container of a single product: order each of their products alone in whole‑container (~ CBM) lots, instead of mixing products. Reduces manual edits for full‑container‑MOQ suppliers.

Lead-time assumptions (days) Goods Ready date = requested arrival week − UK inland transport − production grace − sea sailing. In the exported form these sit in one master cell per value, so the factory (or you) can flex them across the whole sheet at once.
Payment terms & contributions (% of order value) Applied to the exported year's total order value to show the marketing contribution, deposit and resulting final balance in the form's summary.

Every Excel import lives here. Each tile colours itself by how fresh it is for its group's cadence — green = current, amber = one period old, red = older or never uploaded. Click a tile to upload; drag tiles to reorder or move them between groups (a tile's group sets its freshness rule).

Sales channels Customers from the forecasting workbook's CustomerIndex, heaviest first. Assign each to a channel (Marketplace / DSV / Direct / Ex-Works) — the Plan's per-product "⇄ Channels" breakdown and the Summary's channel split follow this mapping. Unassigned customers report as Unmapped.
Manual selling prices Edit any product's ASP by hand — e.g. new products not in the sales file. Previous shows the price before the last update and Var % the change from it (e.g. how the most recent upload moved each price). Hand-edited prices are tagged manual; products still on their original imported price are tagged not updated (and highlighted in the supplier screen). Changes apply to the current year and later forecast years.

Purchase orders & container bookings Upload the WEBSA Open PO and Qlik Container files from the File Imports tab. Here you can view diagnostics and re-time committed stock arrivals to their PO/container weeks (matched by PO number, across all years).
Phase 2: re‑time committed stock arrivals to their PO arrival weeks — the booked Qlik container date, or the PO due date where no container is booked yet — so the plan aligns to the PO/Qlik exports. Preview every move before applying; undo in one click. Stock not on any PO stays put (use the review above).
Stock weeks with no PO match Weeks (from the current week on) where the plan has committed stock but no PO / container booking matched — often because the container was consolidated into another week, or the order hasn't been raised. Review each before Phase 2 re‑times arrivals, so nothing is lost: Review drops you on the supplier at that week (flagged) and lets you cycle through — amend the week or clear the order, then move on. Cycling runs through the current supplier's weeks first, then the next supplier in your sidebar order.
Order data Replace every order quantity in the app with the quantities originally imported from the Excel workbook. Your manual edits will be lost.
Change history Every file upload and data edit, newest first, with a date & time stamp. The ↻ History button in the top bar reverts the most recent changes quickly; the most recent 5 changes can be reverted (older ones stay here for reference). Reverting restores the data exactly as it was immediately before that change.
